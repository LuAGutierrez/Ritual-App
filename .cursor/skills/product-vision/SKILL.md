# Rituales — Product Vision

## Core Concept

Rituales is a mobile-first emotional connection platform initially focused on couples through:
- daily rituals,
- interactive prompts,
- emotional games,
- lightweight connection experiences,
- and habit-driven engagement.

The product is NOT a generic couple questions app.

The long-term vision is to become:
- an emotional connection platform,
- a relationship companion,
- and a scalable social-emotional ecosystem.

The experience must feel:
- intimate,
- warm,
- elegant,
- emotionally safe,
- modern,
- lightweight,
- and calm.

The ideal use case:
"Open Rituales together for 5 minutes before sleep."

---

# Product Philosophy

Rituales should:
- create recurring emotional moments,
- reduce friction,
- encourage vulnerability safely,
- generate anticipation,
- and build emotional habits.

The app should never feel:
- overwhelming,
- therapist-heavy,
- childish,
- noisy,
- or overly gamified.

Avoid:
- excessive notifications,
- aggressive monetization,
- complicated onboarding,
- feature overload.

---

# Core Product Principles

## 1. Simplicity First
Every interaction must feel immediate and intuitive.

## 2. Emotional Warmth
The app should feel human and comforting.

## 3. Short Session Design
Target session duration:
3–5 minutes.

## 4. Shared Participation
Features should encourage both partners to participate.

## 5. Habit Formation
Daily rituals and streaks are central.

## 6. Emotional Reward
Users should leave interactions feeling:
- closer,
- lighter,
- understood,
- or emotionally stimulated.

---

# Core MVP

The MVP centers around:
## "One Daily Ritual"

Each ritual may include:
- a shared question,
- a mini challenge,
- a playful interaction,
- or a reflection.

The ritual unlocks fully when both participants engage.

Core features:
- daily ritual,
- streaks,
- linked partner profiles,
- invitations,
- basic history,
- emotional categories.

---

# Emotional UX Goals

The product should create:
- anticipation,
- curiosity,
- emotional payoff,
- and micro-memorable moments.

Examples:
- waiting for the other person's answer,
- unlocking responses simultaneously,
- surprise rituals,
- relationship recaps,
- emotional milestones.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Future expansion may include:
- AI-driven rituals,
- emotional insights,
- relationship coaching,
- shared journaling,
- friend/group rituals,
- long-distance relationship features,
- social emotional ecosystems,
- real-world integrations.

---

# Success Metric

Primary metric:
## "Completed rituals per couple"

This matters more than:
- downloads,
- MAU,
- or passive engagement.

The goal is active emotional participation.

---

# UX Tone

The UX should feel:
- soft,
- premium,
- calm,
- emotionally intelligent,
- and intentional.

Avoid:
- excessive dopamine mechanics,
- loud animations,
- cluttered screens,
- meme aesthetics,
- hard gamification.
