# Rituales — Growth & Monetization

## Core Strategy

Monetization should never block:
- emotional connection,
- core rituals,
- or habit formation.

Free users must still have a complete emotional experience.

Premium should provide:
- depth,
- personalization,
- exclusivity,
- and emotional richness.

---

# Free Tier

Includes:
- daily rituals,
- basic streaks,
- limited history,
- limited categories,
- couple linking.

Goal:
- retention,
- habit formation,
- viral growth.

---

# Premium Tier

Includes:
- unlimited ritual packs,
- premium categories,
- anniversary rituals,
- relationship milestones,
- advanced history,
- statistics,
- customization,
- seasonal events.

---

# Premium Categories

Examples:
- deep conversations,
- intimacy,
- playful chaos,
- spicy mode,
- emotional vulnerability,
- long-distance relationships,
- reconnection rituals,
- anniversary experiences.

---

# AI Premium Features

AI should feel:
- emotionally aware,
- personalized,
- relational.

Avoid:
- generic chatbot behavior,
- robotic conversations,
- assistant-like UX.

Good AI examples:
- adaptive rituals,
- emotional summaries,
- relationship insights,
- personalized suggestions,
- emotional pattern detection,
- communication coaching.

---

# Core Retention Mechanic

Primary loop:
1. Notification
2. Shared anticipation
3. Ritual completion
4. Emotional reward
5. Streak reinforcement
6. Repeat next day

---

# Key Metrics

Primary metric:
## Completed rituals per couple

Secondary metrics:
- weekly retention,
- streak length,
- partner invitation rate,
- emotional category engagement,
- premium conversion,
- average rituals completed weekly.

---

# Viral Mechanics

Use:
- invite-based onboarding,
- partner unlocking,
- shared screenshots,
- emotional recaps,
- anniversary summaries,
- streak milestones.

Avoid:
- spam referrals,
- aggressive social sharing,
- growth hacks that damage intimacy.

---

# Product Goal

The app should become:
- part of relationship routines,
- emotionally meaningful,
- and difficult to replace emotionally.
